A one-image summary of the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**,
drawn as a newspaper front page (1024×1024 PNG).

**Date rule:** a story is included only if the event happened, or was
first reported, on 24 November 2026 in some time zone. Stories that only
*look* like that day's news are listed in the poster's DATE CHECK box and
deliberately excluded.

## Main stories

1. **Ukraine — Zaporizhzhia:** Russian strike kills 2, wounds 40+;
   a house destroyed and a five-storey apartment block torn into
   (Reuters).
2. **Indonesia — Serang, Banten:** Death toll from the Milkita
   candy-factory fire rises to 26 as two more bodies are retrieved;
   8 still missing (CNA / police). The fire itself began on 21 Nov —
   the 24 Nov news is the rising toll.
3. **South Korea — Ulleung Island:** About 5 Russian warplanes enter
   the KADIZ; Seoul issues the island's first preliminary air-raid
   alert and scrambles F-15Ks (Yonhap).
4. **Saudi Arabia — Riyadh:** Saudi-led coalition says the PIF Tower
   was targeted again in a terrorist attack; no casualty figures
   given (Reuters).
5. **Africa — Sport:** CAS restores Senegal's AFCON title, overturning
   CAF's decision to award Morocco a 3-0 default win (AP).
6. **United States — Economy:** Consumer confidence jumps 11.4 points
   to 103.3, the highest since January 2025 (Conference Board via
   Reuters).

## In brief

- **Hormuz:** Iran *claims* 3 tankers hit naval mines east of the
  strait; talks ruled out until 7 conditions are met (CNBC).
- **Jerusalem:** Eisenkot's Yashar clinches the first coalition deal
  (Reuters).
- **Minsk:** Lukashenko proposes flying with Zelenskyy to Moscow for
  talks (Reuters).
- **Geneva:** World Chess Championship Game 1 opens — Gukesh vs
  Sindarov, both 20, $2.5m fund (ChessBase / lichess).
- **Wall Street:** Nvidia slips ~5% on Tuesday as Monday's surge
  cools (Reuters). The CEO return itself was 23 Nov news.
- **Kourou:** Ariane 6's 10th flight launches 36 Amazon Leo
  satellites (Arianespace).
- **Washington:** Kilmar Abrego Garcia agrees to leave the US
  voluntarily (Reuters).
- **Iowa:** Court clears the Army to detonate ageing toxic explosives
  (Reuters).
- **At sea:** US probes cyberattacks on up to 50 commercial ships
  (WSJ).

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`).
The poster itself was drawn with `build_image.py` (Pillow).
